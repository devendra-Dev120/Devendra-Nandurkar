About the Project

Project Overview

The project analyzes a retail store sales dataset to understand how revenue is distributed across product categories, payment methods, and store types. The analysis also examines transaction volume and average bill value to summarize business performance.

Objective

Identify sales patterns and understand which categories and store types contribute most to total revenue.

Analysis

Summarize revenue, quantities, transactions, payment methods, and category performance.

Insights

Use the results to communicate important patterns in the retail sales data.

What the Data Shows

Key Findings

Electronics Led Revenue
Electronics generated ₹2,96,000, accounting for approximately 77.62% of total sales.
Grocery Had the Highest Quantity
Grocery recorded 108 units sold, the highest quantity among the analyzed product categories.
Mall Stores Led Sales
Mall stores generated ₹3,59,800, contributing approximately 94.35% of total sales.
Payment Method Patterns
Credit cards accounted for the highest sales value, while UPI had the highest transaction count.
